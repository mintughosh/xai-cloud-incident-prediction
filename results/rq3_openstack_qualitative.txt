Qualitative description:

The 4 anomalous OpenStack instances trigger 13 distinct event templates over 100 log lines, and critically, none of the top-occurring templates are explicit error or failure messages — they are routine VM lifecycle operations (instance creation, resource claims, deletion, and network deallocation). All 100/100 lines fall into 'Unknown': not one of the 13 templates these instances triggered carries any failure-specific vocabulary. This indicates that, unlike HDFS and BGL where anomalies correspond to distinct error-type log events, OpenStack's injected anomalies in this dataset manifest as anomalous *timing or sequencing* of otherwise-normal lifecycle events rather than the presence of a distinctly anomalous log line, which independently explains why the event-count feature representation used for RQ1/RQ2 could not separate them (only 4 positives, near-zero F1 across all models) — the signal these templates carry is not really present at the per-template-occurrence level to begin with.
